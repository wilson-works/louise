# Scope Brief: Small-shop bookkeeping software compared

_Wave 2 / 3. Sonnet front-runner audit trail. This is an invented example for Louise's demo library._

## Restated topic

For a one-person craft shop selling online and at weekend markets, how do the three common kinds of bookkeeping
tool (a spreadsheet template, desktop accounting software, and a cloud subscription) compare on cost, effort and
what they handle?

## Sub-questions

1. What does each kind cost over three years?
2. Which ones import bank and card-reader transactions on their own?
3. Which handle sales tax, stock and simple invoices?
4. How hard is it to move to another tool later?

## Suggested sources

- Small-business advice services
- Independent software review sites
- Tax authority guidance for sole traders

## Expected structure

- 01-overview.md: the comparison table and a recommendation by shop size

6

Compare kinds of tool, not named products: prices and features change too often for a product list to stay
current. Alex has about 40 sales a week.
