# Choosing a used e-bike: in depth

> An invented example from Louise's demo library. The sources are made up and live on example.org.

## Battery health in detail

Many e-bike displays or shop tools can report the number of charge cycles; under 300 cycles is a good sign on a
bike up to three years old [^2]. If no tool is to hand, a full charge followed by a known test route gives a rough
range figure to compare with the maker's claim [^2].

A battery stored flat for months can fail to wake up at all; ask how the bike was kept over winter [^3].

## A test-ride routine

1. Start on the lowest assist level and step up one level at a time; each step should feel stronger [^4].
2. Stop on a slope and set off again: a healthy motor gives a firm push within half a pedal turn [^4].
3. Brake hard twice from a moderate speed; e-bikes are heavier, so worn pads show up quickly [^7].

## Paperwork

- A receipt or the original order confirmation helps show the seller owns the bike [^5].
- The frame number can be checked against the example stolen-bike register before any money changes hands [^5].
