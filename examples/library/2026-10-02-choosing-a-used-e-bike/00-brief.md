_Wave 1 / 3. Sonnet front-runner audit trail. This is an invented example for Louise's demo library._

## Restated topic

What should a first-time buyer check before buying a second-hand electric bicycle for a 10 km commute, and what
does a fair price look like?

## Sub-questions

1. How do you judge the health of a used battery, and what does a replacement cost?
2. Which motor and drivetrain checks catch the expensive problems?
3. How fast do e-bikes lose value, and what is a fair asking price by age?
4. What paperwork protects the buyer (proof of purchase, frame number, theft checks)?
