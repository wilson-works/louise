# Scope Brief: Frost dates for raised beds

_Wave 1 / 3. Sonnet front-runner audit trail. This is an invented example for Louise's demo library._

## Restated topic

When is it safe to plant frost-tender vegetables into raised beds in a cool-temperate garden, and how do raised beds
change the timing compared with planting in the ground?

## Sub-questions

1. What is a "last frost date", and how are average dates and their probabilities published?
2. How much earlier do raised beds warm in spring than open ground, and why?
3. Which soil temperatures do common tender crops (tomatoes, beans, squash) need before planting out?
4. What simple protections (covers, cloches) extend the safe window, and by how much?

## Suggested sources

- Regional agricultural extension services
- National weather service climate summaries
- University horticulture departments
