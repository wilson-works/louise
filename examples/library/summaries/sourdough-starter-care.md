# Sourdough Starter Care — Summary

For Alex, who bakes on Saturdays: how to keep a starter healthy with one feed a week. An invented example from
Louise's demo library.

## Key Facts

- At about 21 °C a 1:1:1 feed peaks in 4 to 8 hours; a stiffer 1:2:2 feed slows it down.
  @research/2026-09-14-sourdough-starter-care/01-overview.md
- Grey liquid on top means hungry, not spoiled; pink, orange or fuzzy patches mean throw it out.
  @research/2026-09-14-sourdough-starter-care/01-overview.md
- A month-old fridge starter usually recovers after two or three feeds, 12 hours apart.
  @research/2026-09-14-sourdough-starter-care/01-overview.md

## Recommended Architecture / Decisions

- Fridge all week, one weekly feed; feed 1:2:2 on Friday evening and bake when it has doubled.
