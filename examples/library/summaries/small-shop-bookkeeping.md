# Small-Shop Bookkeeping Software — Summary

For Alex's one-person craft shop (about 40 sales a week, online and at markets): which kind of bookkeeping tool
fits. An invented example from Louise's demo library.

## Key Facts

- Spreadsheets cost least but every import, invoice and tax figure is done by hand.
  @research/2026-10-02-small-shop-bookkeeping-software-compared/01-overview.md
- Cloud subscriptions cost the most over three years and bring automatic bank feeds.
  @research/2026-10-02-small-shop-bookkeeping-software-compared/01-overview.md
- In the example survey, shops over about 30 sales a week saved two to three hours a month with bank feeds.
  @research/2026-10-02-small-shop-bookkeeping-software-compared/01-overview.md

## Recommended Architecture / Decisions

- At about 40 sales a week, a cloud subscription, chosen for its bank feeds and invoices.
