# Choosing a Used E-Bike — Summary

For Alex, before viewing a second-hand e-bike for a 10 km commute with one long hill. An invented example from
Louise's demo library.

## Key Facts

- The battery is usually the most expensive part to replace, often a third or more of the bike's used value.
  @research/2026-10-02-choosing-a-used-e-bike/01-overview.md
- Batteries keep about 80 percent of their capacity for 500 to 1,000 full charges; under 300 cycles is a good sign
  on a bike up to three years old. @research/2026-10-02-choosing-a-used-e-bike/02-deep-dive.md
- E-bikes lose about 20 to 30 percent of their value in year one and about 10 percent a year after (one example
  survey). @research/2026-10-02-choosing-a-used-e-bike/01-overview.md

## Recommended Architecture / Decisions

- Judge the battery first, then ride it up a hill on full assist, then check the paperwork.
- Offer about half the new price for a three-year-old bike in good order.
